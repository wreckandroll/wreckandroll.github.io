Honda Civic head unit, listed on eBay

A used 7-inch touchscreen head unit with sat nav, removed from a 2020 Honda Civic RS, taken from the yard’s inventory to a live listing on eBay Australia.

Real job for an Australian auto wrecker, shared with their permission. Stock numbers, tag numbers, VIN and listing ID are hidden. Tap any screenshot to see it larger.

How we built the listing

  1. Category. Vehicle Parts & Accessories > Vehicle Electronics & GPS > Car Audio > Car Audio In-Dash Units, so the part shows up where buyers look for it.
  2. Photos. 7 photos uploaded, with the clearest one chosen as the main photo.
  3. Price and format. Buy It Now at the yard’s price of AU $400, using the yard’s business policies.
  4. Description. States the donor vehicle and keeps the yard’s stock and tag numbers, so the part is easy to find in the yard when it sells.
  5. Compatibility. Fitment added for 11 vehicles, matched to the donor vehicle. Buyers are asked to confirm the part number before buying.
    YearModelBodyEngine
    2016Honda Civic RSSedan1.5L 4cyl Turbo Petrol
    2017 to 2021Honda Civic RSSedan and Hatchback1.5L 4cyl Turbo Petrol
  6. Live. The listing went live with a search-friendly title covering model, generation, engine and years.

The result

  • Search-friendly title with model, generation, engine and years
  • Correct eBay category: Car Audio In-Dash Units
  • Stock and tag numbers recorded, so the part is easy to find
  • Vehicle compatibility added for exact fitment
  • Buy It Now with the yard’s business policies
