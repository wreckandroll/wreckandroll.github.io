Volkswagen Golf door mirror, listed on eBay

A used left door mirror (manual fold, 6 wire) from a Volkswagen Golf, listed on eBay Australia with its OEM part number, full item specifics and fitment.

Real job for an Australian auto wrecker, shared with their permission. Stock numbers, tag numbers and listing ID are hidden. Tap any screenshot to see it larger.

How we built the listing

  1. Title. 74 of 80 characters: make, model, side, generation, fold type with its equipment code (6XN), wiring and years. The yard’s stock and tag numbers go in the Custom label (SKU), so the part is easy to find when it sells.
  2. Category. Mirror Assemblies, under Vehicle Parts & Accessories > Car & Truck Parts & Accessories > Exterior Parts & Accessories > Side View Mirrors.
  3. Item specifics. Brand, colour, number of pieces, placement, features, mirror adjustment method and type, plus the OEM part number 5G2 857 507 BN in both Manufacturer Part Number and Reference OE/OEM Number, so buyers searching by number find it.
  4. Compatibility. Volkswagen Golf FWD Diesel 2.0L 4cyl 103kW (engine codes CBAB, CFFB, CJAA), Hatchback 2.0 TDI (5K1), all 6 trims from 2008 to 2013.

The result

  • Search-friendly title using most of eBay’s 80 characters
  • OEM part number in both part number fields
  • Item specifics filled for the details buyers filter by
  • Fitment matched to engine codes and body
  • Stock and tag numbers kept in the SKU for the yard
