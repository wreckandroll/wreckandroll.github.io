What we do

Listing creation

New listings from your photos and part details, with photos chosen and ordered so the part is clear.

Titles

Make, model, year range, side, part name and part number, written the way buyers search.

Descriptions

Clear condition notes, fitment information and what's included, so buyers know exactly what they get.

Item specifics

Brand, manufacturer part number (MPN), placement on vehicle and other specifics filled in completely.

Updating existing listings

Improving weak titles, missing specifics and incomplete descriptions on listings you already have.

Quantity and sold items

Updating quantities and ending listings for parts that have sold or been scrapped, so you don't sell what you don't have.

Pinnacle Professional or direct eBay

Listing through Pinnacle Professional

If your yard lists to eBay through Pinnacle, we enter the part details, part numbers and descriptions in Pinnacle and follow your yard's listing process.

Listing directly on eBay

If you list straight on eBay, we work in your seller account, creating and updating listings with the access you approve.

We work within your current setup and don't change how your systems are connected.

Our checks for every listing

  • Part number found from the vehicle's VIN, using Partsouq for Japanese makes and Partslink24 for European makes
  • Correct side confirmed (left or right, driver or passenger)
  • Variants checked, for example blind spot, camera, indicator, heated or power folding mirrors
  • Condition noted honestly, including any damage visible in photos
  • Part number recorded in the title, item specifics and description
What we need from you
  • Photos of each part (taken at your yard)
  • The donor vehicle's VIN or stock details
  • Access to Pinnacle Professional or your eBay account, through a remote session you approve
  • Your pricing rules, or prices for each part

Questions about eBay listings

Do you work in Pinnacle Professional or directly on eBay?

Either. If your yard lists through Pinnacle Professional, we work inside Pinnacle following your yard's process. If you list directly on eBay, we work in your eBay seller account. We work within the setup you already have and don't change how your systems are connected.

Who takes the photos?

You do. Photos and dismantling have to happen at your yard. We take it from there: choosing and ordering photos, finding part numbers, writing listings and keeping records up to date.

What exactly is in the free trial?

Up to 25 parts processed free: new eBay listings, inventory records, or a mix. It's completed within 5 business days of receiving what we need, you check everything before it goes live, and there's no payment or obligation.

Do you guarantee more sales?

No. Sales depend on your stock, prices and demand. What we can promise is accurate, complete listings and stock records, done consistently.
