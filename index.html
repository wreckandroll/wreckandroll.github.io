Frequently asked questions

Who do you work with?

Our main clients are auto wreckers and dismantlers that sell used parts online in Australia, New Zealand, the UK and Ireland. We also work with salvage yards, used auto parts sellers and other online sellers with large parts catalogues.

Do you work in Pinnacle Professional or directly on eBay?

Either. If your yard lists through Pinnacle Professional, we work inside Pinnacle following your yard's process. If you list directly on eBay, we work in your eBay seller account. We work within the setup you already have and don't change how your systems are connected.

Who takes the photos?

You do. Photos and dismantling have to happen at your yard. We take it from there: choosing and ordering photos, finding part numbers, writing listings and keeping records up to date.

How do you access our systems?

Through remote desktop tools such as TeamViewer or HelpWire. You start or approve every session, give us a user account with only the access the job needs, and can remove access at any time.

Is our data kept confidential?

Yes. We sign a confidentiality agreement (NDA) before starting, never share your stock, prices or customer details, and delete any working files when the work ends. We don't access banking, payroll or email.

What exactly is in the free trial?

Up to 25 parts processed free: new eBay listings, inventory records, or a mix. It's completed within 5 business days of receiving what we need, you check everything before it goes live, and there's no payment or obligation.

How is the work priced after the trial?

Per listing or per hour, whichever suits your yard, billed monthly after the work is done. There's no lock-in contract. We'll give you a clear quote after the trial.

What hours do you work?

Your business hours. We're based in Islamabad, Pakistan, and schedule our work around Australian, New Zealand, UK and Irish trading hours.

Do you guarantee more sales?

No. Sales depend on your stock, prices and demand. What we can promise is accurate, complete listings and stock records, done consistently.
