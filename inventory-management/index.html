What we do

Stock entry

Entering new parts into your yard system, organised by category such as engine, transmission, front end, doors and electrical.

OEM part numbers

Finding the correct part number from the donor vehicle's VIN and recording it against each part.

SKU and location organisation

Consistent stock numbers, SKUs and yard locations, so parts can be found quickly when they sell.

Quantity updates

Updating quantities as parts sell, are scrapped or are added, so your records match what's on the shelf.

Record cleanup

Fixing duplicate, missing and outdated records, including incomplete descriptions and condition notes.

Inventory accuracy checks

Regular checks that your stock records and online listings match, with updates where they don't.

Questions about inventory management

Do you work in Pinnacle Professional or directly on eBay?

Either. If your yard lists through Pinnacle Professional, we work inside Pinnacle following your yard's process. If you list directly on eBay, we work in your eBay seller account. We work within the setup you already have and don't change how your systems are connected.

How do you access our systems?

Through remote desktop tools such as TeamViewer or HelpWire. You start or approve every session, give us a user account with only the access the job needs, and can remove access at any time.

Is our data kept confidential?

Yes. We sign a confidentiality agreement (NDA) before starting, never share your stock, prices or customer details, and delete any working files when the work ends. We don't access banking, payroll or email.

How is the work priced after the trial?

Per listing or per hour, whichever suits your yard, billed monthly after the work is done. There's no lock-in contract. We'll give you a clear quote after the trial.
