eBay fees on car parts: how returns can raise them by 5.5 points

Most yards know eBay takes a cut. Fewer know that the fee can rise when too many buyers return parts as “not as described”. Here’s how it works, what it costs, and how to keep it from happening.

Fees and rules from eBay Australia’s help pages, checked October 2026. eBay changes fees from time to time, so confirm current rates on eBay’s selling fees page.

The normal fee on vehicle parts

eBay charges a final value fee when an item sells, calculated on the total sale amount including postage, plus a small fixed fee per order. For eBay Store subscribers, the rate in Vehicle Parts & Accessories categories is between 10.56% and 12.43%, depending on the store package.

When the fee goes up

eBay adds 5.5 percentage points to the variable final value fee if:

  • Your seller level is evaluated as Below Standard (applies to all your sales), or
  • Your ‘item not as described’ return rate is Very High in a category (applies to sales in that category)

The higher rate applies to sales in the calendar month after the evaluation.

Why parts get returned as “not as described”

  • Wrong side: a left part listed or shipped as right
  • Wrong version: the same part for a different trim, engine or year range
  • Missing details: features, connectors or damage not mentioned
  • Photos that don’t match the part that was shipped
  • Wrong part picked from the shelf because stock records are wrong

How to prevent it

  1. Use the OEM part number to confirm the exact version. See how to find OEM part numbers by VIN.
  2. Check the side from the driver’s seat. See driver side vs passenger side.
  3. Fill item specifics from the actual part. See how to fill eBay item specifics.
  4. Add accurate compatibility, so “doesn’t fit” returns are covered by eBay Guaranteed Fit and don’t count against you. See eBay Guaranteed Fit for wreckers.
  5. Keep stock records accurate, so the part that ships is the part in the listing. See how to inventory a vehicle part by part.

Keep an eye on your service metrics

eBay shows your ‘item not as described’ rate and how it compares with similar sellers in Seller Hub, under performance and service metrics. Checking it monthly lets you fix a problem category before the higher fee applies.

Frequently asked questions

What are eBay’s fees for car parts in Australia?

For eBay Store subscribers, the final value fee in Vehicle Parts & Accessories categories is between 10.56% and 12.43%, depending on the store package, plus a small fixed fee per order. Check eBay’s fee pages for current rates.

Why did my eBay fees go up?

If your seller level is Below Standard, or your ‘item not as described’ return rate is Very High in a category, eBay adds 5.5 percentage points to the variable final value fee for sales in that category.

How do I lower my ‘not as described’ returns on car parts?

List the right side and version, use the OEM part number, show photos of the actual part, add accurate compatibility so Guaranteed Fit applies, and make sure the right part is picked and shipped.
