eBay Guaranteed Fit: what it means for auto wreckers

eBay Australia changed how “doesn’t fit” returns work for vehicle parts. For yards that sell on eBay, accurate compatibility is now more than a sales tool: it decides which returns count against you. Here’s what changed and what to do about it.

Based on eBay Australia’s seller protections policy, checked October 2026. eBay updates its policies, so confirm the latest details on eBay’s seller protections page.

What changed

From 31 March 2026, buyers on eBay.com.au can only report that a part doesn’t fit their vehicle if the purchase was covered by eBay Guaranteed Fit.

A purchase is covered when the buyer’s vehicle details match the compatibility information (fitment) you added to the listing. The buyer then sees a green Guaranteed Fit tick when buying.

How it protects you

When a buyer returns a Guaranteed Fit purchase because it doesn’t fit their vehicle, eBay says it will:

  • Cover the cost of an eBay return label (where the item is eligible for one)
  • Not count the return in your ‘not as described’ rate in service metrics
  • Remove negative or neutral feedback left for that sale

That matters because a ‘Very High’ not-as-described rate raises your eBay fees in that category. See how returns can raise your eBay fees.

What this means for a wrecking yard

Your listingsWhat happens
Accurate compatibility, matched to the donor carBuyers see the green tick, and “doesn’t fit” returns are protected
No compatibility addedBuyers can’t use Guaranteed Fit, and fewer buyers searching by their car will find the part
Too many vehicles added (over-fitting)More covered sales to cars the part doesn’t really fit, meaning more returns to handle and parts sitting in transit

The goal is compatibility that is complete but accurate: every vehicle the part fits, and no more.

Returns Guaranteed Fit doesn’t cover

Other return reasons work as before. If a buyer says the part doesn’t match the description or photos, or the wrong item was sent, that can still count against you. So the basics still matter:

  • The right side (left or right, taken from the driver’s seat). See driver side vs passenger side.
  • The right version, checked with the OEM part number. See how to find OEM part numbers by VIN.
  • Photos of the actual part, showing any damage
  • The right part picked from the shelf, using accurate stock and tag numbers

Also note eBay’s rule: never advise a buyer to choose “doesn’t fit my vehicle” when they are returning for a different reason.

How to add compatibility well

  1. Start from the donor car: make, model, variant, engine and body from the vehicle record.
  2. Use the catalogue’s date range for the part number to decide which years to include.
  3. Match engine codes and body types, not just the model name.
  4. Don’t tick “Universal Fitment” for parts that only fit certain vehicles.
  5. Check it: enter the donor car’s details in eBay’s fit check and make sure it shows as a fit.

eBay lets you add up to 3,000 compatible vehicles per listing from its master vehicle list, and add compatibility in bulk through Seller Hub reports.

Quick checklist

  • Compatibility added to every listing in a compatibility-enabled category
  • Vehicles matched to engine code, body and the catalogue date range
  • No universal fitment on vehicle-specific parts
  • Side, part number and photos checked
  • Stock and tag numbers correct, so the right part ships

Frequently asked questions

What is eBay Guaranteed Fit?

An eBay Australia protection for vehicle parts. When a buyer’s vehicle matches the compatibility information in your listing, they see a green Guaranteed Fit tick when buying.

What happens if a Guaranteed Fit part doesn’t fit?

The buyer can return it as “doesn’t fit my vehicle”. eBay covers the return label where available, the return isn’t counted in your ‘not as described’ rate, and negative or neutral feedback for the sale is removed.

Can buyers still return a part as “doesn’t fit” without Guaranteed Fit?

Since 31 March 2026, buyers can only report that an item doesn’t fit their vehicle if the purchase was covered by eBay Guaranteed Fit.
