Sales support for auto wreckers: quotes, orders and dispatch

For yards that use Pinnacle Professional and can’t answer every part enquiry quickly. We reply to customers and other yards by message, complete the sale in Pinnacle, and make sure your team gets the part ready for dispatch or pickup.

How it works

  1. A customer or another yard asks for a part. We reply by message and send the quote in the chat.
  2. The customer confirms. We complete the sale in Pinnacle, using the login you give us.
  3. Your team gets the part ready. We tell your staff which part to prepare.
  4. Delivery or pickup. For delivery, we pass the customer’s details to your dispatch team. For pickup, we let your team know so the part is ready when the customer arrives.

What you get

  • Faster replies to part enquiries, so customers don’t buy elsewhere while they wait
  • Every sale recorded in Pinnacle, so sold parts don’t show as available
  • Clear instructions for your team: which part to prepare, and whether it ships or is collected
  • More time for your staff to dismantle, pick and pack

Who we deal with

Retail customers and trade buyers, including other wrecking yards, the same people who already contact your yard. We follow your prices and policies, and check with you before anything outside them.

How access works

NDA signed before we start. We use the Pinnacle login you give us, ideally one with only the access the job needs. No access to banking, payroll or email. More: Pinnacle Professional help.

Pricing

AUD 15 to 18 per hour, or a fixed monthly fee for a set amount of work. See all pricing.

Frequently asked questions

Do you talk to our customers?

Yes, by message. We reply to part enquiries from customers and other yards, send the quote in the chat, and confirm the order with them.

What happens after the customer confirms?

We complete the sale in Pinnacle, then ask your team to get the part ready. For delivery, we pass the customer’s details to your dispatch team; for pickup, we let your team know so the part is ready when the customer arrives.

Do you need access to our banking?

No. We work in Pinnacle with the login you give us, and never need access to banking, payroll or email.
